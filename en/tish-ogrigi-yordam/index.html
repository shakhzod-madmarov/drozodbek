Safe Temporary Steps Before Your Appointment

  • ✓Rinse gently with warm salt water (1/2 tsp salt in a glass of warm water) to soothe irritated gum tissue.
  • ✓Gently floss around the aching tooth to dislodge trapped food particles.
  • ✓Take an over-the-counter pain reliever such as ibuprofen or acetaminophen if medically safe for you.
  • ✓Apply a cold compress on the outside cheek for 10–15 minutes to reduce swelling.

!DANGEROUS MYTHS TO STRICTLY AVOID!

  • ✕NEVER place crushed aspirin, alcohol, vinegar, or garlic directly onto the tooth or gum — this causes severe CHEMICAL BURNS.
  • ✕NEVER apply heat packs to a swollen jaw — heat rapidly worsens dental abscesses and spreading infection.
  • ✕Do not delay treatment by relying solely on leftover antibiotics.

Symptom Decoder: Identify Your Dental Issue

Match what you are feeling to understand the cause and clinical cure:

01

Throbbing toothache that worsens in the evening or when lying down

Likely Cause:

Acute Pulpitis (Dental Nerve Inflammation)

Deep decay has reached the dental pulp. Lying down increases cranial blood pressure around the inflamed nerve.

Clinical Treatment:

Gentle root canal relief under two-stage anesthesia (open until 22:00).

02

Sharp pain triggered by cold drinks, hot tea, or sweets

Likely Cause:

Dental Caries (Cavity) or Micro-fracture

Eroded enamel exposes sensitive dentin tubules directly to thermal and sugary triggers.

Clinical Treatment:

30-minute decay excavation and German/Japanese composite restoration.

View Treatment →
03

Lost filling or chipped front / chewing tooth

Likely Cause:

Secondary decay or weakened enamel fracture

Exposed inner dentin traps bacteria and risks deeper tooth fracture when chewing.

Clinical Treatment:

Same-day bonding repair or Shining 3D scanned zirconia crown.

View Treatment →
04

Swollen gum/cheek (dental abscess) or pain when biting down

Likely Cause:

Periodontitis, periapical abscess, or impacted wisdom tooth

Infection has spread beyond the root tip. Applying heat can worsen swelling rapidly.

Clinical Treatment:

Immediate exam: safe drainage, root retreatment, or atraumatic extraction.

View Treatment →
05

Bleeding gums when brushing and persistent bad breath

Likely Cause:

Subgingival tartar buildup and gingivitis

Calcified plaque irritates gums continuously, causing inflammation and eventual tooth loosening.

Clinical Treatment:

30-minute gentle ultrasonic scaling & AirFlow polishing.

View Treatment →
06

Missing tooth causing chewing trouble or smile gaps

Likely Cause:

Tooth loss risking bone atrophy and shifting teeth

Without a root in the jawbone, bone volume shrinks and neighboring teeth tilt inward.

Clinical Treatment:

15–20 minute MegaGen biocompatible titanium implant with lifetime warranty.
